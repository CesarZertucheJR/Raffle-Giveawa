🎁 Giveaway Wheel

Grab the comments from your giveaway posts, and everyone who commented goes on the wheel. Then spin for a winner!

1 Add the “Grab comments” button (one time only)

Use a computer for this (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

  1. Make sure your bookmarks bar is showing at the top of the browser. If it isn’t, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or ⌘+Shift+B (Mac).
  2. Click and hold this button, then drag it up onto the bookmarks bar and let go:
    🎁 Grab comments
  3. That’s it! You’ll now see 🎁 Grab comments in your bookmarks bar.
Dragging doesn’t work?
  1. Click
  2. Right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page… (or Add bookmark).
  3. Name it Grab comments, and in the URL box paste what you copied. Save.

2 Grab the comments from each giveaway post

  1. Open your giveaway post on instagram.com or facebook.com (be logged in like normal). On Facebook, click the post’s date so it opens by itself.
  2. Click 🎁 Grab comments in your bookmarks bar.
  3. Wait while it opens all the comments, then click Send to Giveaway Wheel.

Do this for each giveaway post. Doing it again on the same post just updates it with the newest comments.
