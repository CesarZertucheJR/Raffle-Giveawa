❓ How do I copy the likes and comments?

Tip: this is easiest on a computer (not a phone), at instagram.com or facebook.com.

📸 Instagram

  1. Click Open post below the link.
  2. Likes: click the “___ likes” text under the photo. A list pops up. Scroll to the very bottom so everyone loads. Then click just before the first name, hold the mouse down, and drag to the last name. Press Ctrl+C (⌘+C on Mac) and paste into the Likes box.
  3. Comments: keep clicking the ⊕ button until all comments show. Highlight them all the same way, copy, and paste into the Comments box.

👍 Facebook

  1. Click Open post below the link.
  2. Likes: click the reaction icons/number (like 👍❤️ 25). A list pops up. Scroll to the bottom so everyone loads, then highlight, copy, and paste into the Likes box.
  3. Comments: change “Most relevant” to All comments, click “View more comments” until all are showing, then highlight, copy, and paste into the Comments box.

Don’t worry about copying extra stuff like “Follow”, “Reply” or “2d”. The site ignores it.
