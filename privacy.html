Privacy

Giveaway Wheel is a personal tool used by its owner to pick giveaway winners from comments on their own Facebook Page and Instagram posts.

  • When you log in with Facebook, the site reads the list of your Pages, your linked Instagram account, your recent posts, and the comments on the posts you choose.
  • This information is only used to show the giveaway entrants and pick a winner. It is kept in your own web browser (local storage) and is never sent to any other server.
  • The site never posts, comments, or changes anything on your accounts.
  • To delete the data, log out and clear this site’s data in your browser, or remove the app under Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites.
