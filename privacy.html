Privacy

Giveaway Wheel is a personal tool for picking giveaway winners from the comments on your own Instagram and Facebook posts.

  • The “Grab comments” button only reads the post you have open in your own browser, when you click it.
  • The names and comments it collects are kept only in your own web browser (local storage). They are never sent to any server.
  • The site never posts, comments, or changes anything on your accounts, and never sees your password.
  • To delete everything, remove the posts on the site or clear this site’s data in your browser.
